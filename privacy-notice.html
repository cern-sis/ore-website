Privacy Notice

This privacy notice concerns the "Open Research Europe publishing platform", referred to as "ORE" in the following.

This privacy notice explains how ORE uses the personal data that is collected from you when you use our website (either as a registered user or as a viewer). In particular, it explains the reason for the processing, the way we collect, handle and ensure protection of all personal data provided, how that information is used and what rights you may exercise in relation to your data (the right to access, rectify, block etc.).

ORE hosted and operated at CERN (the Data Controller or DC). The ORE platform is subject to CERN's special legal status as an Intergovernmental Organization (IGO) and thus enjoys certain privileges and immunities under international law. Processing of personal data at CERN is governed by CERN's Operational Circular 11 (OC11) that offers data protection at the same high standards and is comparable to EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you have any questions about ORE's privacy notice, please don't hesitate to contact us.

How is your data used

Each service at CERN is responsible for compiling its own privacy notice regarding the data it processes.

This privacy notice is part of CERN's Layered Privacy Notice and details the processing that is unique to CERN. It does not address processing by other services on which this service may rely and which have their own privacy notice.

Automated decision making

What automated decision making or profiling is being done by ORE with your personal data:

Personal Data Automated decision making or Profiling Purpose
Data in categories User and Linked accounts When you create an account or change your email address, we match your email address domain against our database of institutional email domains to infer your institutional affiliation. We use the institutional affiliation to automatically set your account status to verified; When you submit a record we check the record with an automatic spam classifier which will inactivate your account and delete the record, if found to be spam with a high probability. We train the spam classifier with manually classified records as well as the data of the user who uploaded the given record. To determine your account status (blocked, verified, or unverified); To prevent spam and misuse of the ORE platform; To monitor compliance with ORE policies; Used to automatically verify an account; Used to automatically flag an account for review by ORE service administrators; Used for training of automatic spam classifier for automatic detection and removal of spam records. You can object to all automatic decisions by contacting us on support.
